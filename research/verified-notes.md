## IMSA — https://www.imsa.edu/admissions/dates-and-deadlines/
- Residential public academy, grades 10–12. Apply in 8th or 9th grade (10th–12th not eligible). Illinois residents (separate out-of-state path).
- Needs Algebra 1 + a HS science course; SAT or ACT scores required (no minimum).
- No tuition for IL residents; annual sliding-scale student fee by income; seat deposit waived for free/reduced lunch.
- Class of 2030: application opens Oct 26, 2026; in-state closes Feb 5, 2027; decisions Apr 5, 2027.
